# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Task Tracker to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project

**Don't:**

- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances
- Troll, insult, make derogatory comments, or attack people personally or politically
- Harass anyone, in public or in private
- Publish others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is inappropriate.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Parv17k). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
